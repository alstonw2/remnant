There is a version of you
that exists only today.

Not the one in your photographs, and not the one anyone will remember — the one in the middle of whatever you are in the middle of. Four questions, one person it is addressed to, sealed until a condition you choose. Asked again next month, and the month after, until the record has built itself out of the answers.

Example
One sitting, finished
Ten minutes of someone's evening, sealed for their daughter to open at the age they wrote it. The person is invented — the twenty-eight-year record behind See what this becomes is real, and the page says which is which.
Note the second question. The model asked no follow-up there and said why — it is allowed to decline, and the refusal is shown rather than hidden.
Everything stays in this browser. Nothing is uploaded except the words you answer with, and only to generate the follow-up questions.

Step one

Who is this for?

One person, by name. Not "my family" — a record addressed to everyone is written to no one, and you can hear it in the writing.

Step two

And how old are you today?

Every entry is stamped with your age, not just the date. It is the only index two people's records ever share — so that one day this can be opened by someone at the age you were when you wrote it, rather than on a calendar date that happens to land on a teenager.
