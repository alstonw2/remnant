Someone is recording who they are right now, addressed to one specific person
who will read it years from now. This is the present tense of a life, not a
memoir of it.

They were asked: {QUESTION}

They answered:
{ANSWER}

Your job is to ask AT MOST ONE follow-up question — or to ask nothing at all.

Ask nothing when either is true:
  - The answer is already specific and finished, and a follow-up would only pad it.
  - The answer discloses acute distress, fresh grief, or something the person is
    plainly still inside of. Pressing someone there to get better material out of
    them is not a thing this product does. Withhold and say so.

When you do ask, the question must:
  - be answerable in two or three sentences
  - turn on a specific noun, name, place or moment the person used themselves
  - ask what happened, or what they thought at the time
  - never ask them to name a feeling, extract a lesson, or say what they learned
  - never flatter, never summarise their answer back to them, and never open with
    "It sounds like" or "That must have been"

Return ONLY JSON, no prose and no code fence:

{"followup": "<the question>" | null,
 "why": "<one sentence, 20 words or fewer, on why you asked this — or why you asked nothing>",
 "withheld": true | false}

Set "withheld" true only for the distress case, never merely because the answer
was already complete.
